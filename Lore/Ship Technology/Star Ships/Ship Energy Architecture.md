## Segment 1 — Primary Reactor Scaling, Containment & Thermal Density

### 1.1 Core Volumetric Density Cap (The 20kt Rule)

All civilian and freelance starships in Federation jurisdiction use hybrid Fusion-Fission Pair Cores (Magnetically Confined Deuterium-Tritium / $^{235}\text{U}/^{238}\text{U}$ Seeded Pinch Drives). These cores emit high-flux fast neutron radiation, prompt gamma spikes, and immense thermal waste during continuous ignition. Reactor density is bounded by hull mass, core spacing, and thermal sinking capacity.

- **Statutory Core Ratio:** No vessel may install or operate more than three ($3$) Primary Hybrid Cores per $20.0\text{ kt}$ of dry hull mass ($0.15\text{ cores/kt}$ maximum).
    
- **Volumetric Mass Sinking:** Every primary core requires $\ge 3.5\text{ kt}$ of non-habitable, high-density structural ballast (tungsten-lithium composite, lead-bismuth thermal channels, sacrificial coolant jackets) wrapped around the engine bed to absorb radiation spikes and thermal soak.
    

#### 1.1.1 Fusion Fuel & Reaction Cell Storage

- **D-T Storage:** Primary fusion fuel stored in magnetically isolated, cryogenic vacuum-jacketed pressure cells at the aft structural keel. Cryo-refrigeration must maintain $\le 19.8\text{ K}$ with quadruple redundant power feeds from the primary battery bus.
    
- **Reaction Cell Cassettes:** Replacement magnetic pinch sleeves and consumable ignition targets housed in automated, armored cassette racks adjacent to core containment. Manual handling of unshielded reaction cells is prohibited during operations.
    

#### 1.1.2 TPC Array Standoff & Thermal Gap

- **Optico-Thermal Standoff:** TPC solid-state photon-harvesting panels must maintain axisymmetric standoff of exactly $1.85\text{ m} \pm 0.05\text{ m}$ from the outer boundary of the primary plasma chamber.
    
- **Flux Uniformity:** The $1.85\text{ m}$ vacuum gap serves as the primary infrared radiation transport corridor. TPC panels must be actively cooled via liquid-lithium heat pipes to maintain semiconductor junction temperatures below $1,400\text{ K}$, ensuring $\ge 42\%$ electrical conversion efficiency.
    

#### 1.1.3 Fissile Fuel Charge Storage & EMC Standardization

- **50% Eutectic Salt Feedstock Rule:** Fissile seed fuel must consist exclusively of $50\%\ \text{U-235/238}$ Fluoride Eutectic Salt Matrix ($5\text{N EMC-Grade}$, $99.999\%$ purity) in liquid lithium-salt carrier. Carriage of raw metallic uranium ore, enriched billets, or un-complexed fissile powders on civilian/freelance hulls is a Class-S felony under Federation Custody Laws.
    
- **Anti-Criticality Vaults:** Salt feedstock tanks ($10.0\text{ t}$ baseline on Nimbus-class) must be segmented into sub-critical geometry cells with fixed neutron-poison baffles (cadmium-hafnium matrix). Tanks must feature automated emergency dump manifolds capable of venting liquid fuel slurry through aft Orion vents within $1.5\text{ s}$ of thermal runaway alert.
    

#### 1.1.4 Spent Slurry & Waste Transfer

- **Automated Extraction:** Fission product slag and irradiated carrier salts must be automatically extracted from the pinch chamber during maintenance cycles via high-temperature electromagnetic MHD pumps.
    
- **Waste Encasing:** Extracted waste must be channeled into vitrified boron-silicate canisters housed within the aft ballast block. Un-encased liquid waste transfer through crew-accessible or midship bulkheads is prohibited by physical pipe routing law.
    

#### 1.1.5 Reactor Spacing & Field Interference

- **Minimum Inter-Core Standoff:** Adjacent hybrid cores on multi-reactor vessels must maintain $\ge 12.0\text{ m}$ center-to-center axial separation.
    
- **Magnetic Decoupling:** Containment coils must be pulse-synchronized or phase-shifted by $120^\circ$ to prevent harmonic resonance vibration from fracturing surrounding Beta-9 Titanium ringframes.
    

#### 1.1.6 Intermediate Electrical Coupling

- **Direct Grid Tapping Prohibited:** Primary hybrid reactors generate zero direct household AC/DC current. All kinetic, thermal, and radiant energy must pass through the TPC photon-absorption loop into hybrid supercapacitor and main battery banks (Segment 2) prior to distribution across the ship bus or ISCD radial stroke coils.
    

### 1.2 Aft Radiation & Thermal Exclusion Zone (AR-TEZ)

The posterior $50\%$ of any starship's total volumetric footprint is legally classified as an Active Radiation & Thermal Exclusion Zone due to unshielded thermodynamic dumping by hybrid fusion-fission pairs.

- **No Personnel Habitation** aft of the midpoint structural bulkhead.
    
- **Open Radiation Exhaust:** Core heat and prompt radiation vent directly through the open-frame aft lattice via Orion-class plasma jet vents and thermal radiators.
    
- **Forward Attenuation Shielding:** A shadow shield (borated polyethylene, depleted uranium, liquid water channels) of $\ge 1.2\text{ m}$ thickness must separate the Aft Engineering Zone from the Forward Habitable Zone.
    

## Segment 2 — TPC Intermediate Conversion Loop

### 2.1 The Intermediate Pre-Charge Requirement

Direct line tapping from reactor cores to general ship systems is strictly illegal under DSPL-PWR-782.

```
+------------------+     Thermal Radiant     +------------------+      DC Electrical     +------------------+
|   Hybrid Core    | ----------------------> |    TPC Array     | ---------------------> | Primary Storage  |
| (Fusion-Fission) |  (IR/UV Photon Flux)    |   (Solid State)  |     (206 Wh/kg)        | (Capacitor/Batt) |
+------------------+                         +------------------+                        +------------------+
```

- **Intermediate Conversion:** Core energy extracted as high-intensity IR/UV thermal radiance from the primary reaction containment lining.
    
- **TPC Absorption:** Solid-state TPC arrays absorb radiant photon flux, converting to raw DC electrical power at $\ge 42\%$ efficiency.
    
- **Mandatory Pre-Charge Loop:** All TPC-generated electrical energy must feed into Hybrid Supercapacitor and Battery Storage Banks before routing to general ship systems, life support, or ISCD actuation coils.
    

### 2.2 Battery Bank & Storage Lading Baselines

Vessels must maintain continuous battery storage capacity proportional to ISCD ring count and reactor output for emergency life support, system boot, and ISCD radial stroke braking.

#### Hybrid Energy Storage System (HESS) Architecture

The vessel's energy storage employs a three-layer HESS to balance energy density (long-term storage) and power density (instantaneous discharge), resolving the Ragone Plot constraint in high-demand starship operations.

##### Layer 1: Primary Battery Bank (Chemical Backbone)

|   |   |   |
|---|---|---|
|**Metric / Parameter**|**Baseline (Nimbus)**|**Clearance**|
|**Capacity**|$10\text{ MWh}$|$25\text{ MWh}$|
|**Mass**|$28.6\text{ t}$|$71.5\text{ t}$|
|**Energy Density**|$\sim 350\text{ Wh/kg}$|$\sim 350\text{ Wh/kg}$|

- **Technology:** Advanced solid-state chemical batteries (lithium-sulfur or graphene-matrix).
    
- **Function:** Long-term energy reservoir for life support, system boot, and sustained operations.
    
- **Operational Limit:** $80\%$ usable capacity ($20\%$ emergency reserve).
    
- **Sprint Capability:** $\sim 5$ consecutive $26\text{-second}$ full-power ISCD sprints at baseline; $\sim 14$ with upgrade.
    

##### Layer 2: Pseudocapacitor Interface (Asymmetric Redox Capacitors)

- **Function:** Intermediate buffer absorbing voltage spikes between bulk storage (Layer 1) and high-demand systems (Layer 3).
    
- **Technology:** Asymmetric redox capacitors with fast surface chemical reactions.
    
- **Integration:** Thin chemical-skin linings wrapped directly around power bus routing channels (no dedicated cargo space).
    
- **Role:** Bridges Layer 1's slow discharge with Layer 3's instantaneous demand, stabilizing power delivery.
    

##### Layer 3: Hybrid Supercapacitor Array (Electrostatic Hammer)

- **Function:** Instantaneous peak power delivery and regenerative braking absorption.
    
- **Technology:** Space-grade lithium-free hybrid supercapacitors.
    
- **Specification:** $206\text{ Wh/kg}$ ($59.74\text{ Wh/L}$, $3.3\text{ V}$ baseline cell).
    
- **Integration:** Distributed throughout $2\text{-meter}$ radial maintenance crawl spaces surrounding ISCD rings.
    
- **Mass:** $\sim 550\text{ kg}$ per $200\text{ rings}$.
    
- **Role:** Handles $699.84\text{ MW}$ instantaneous peak power demand (Nimbus-class ISCD); absorbs regenerative braking surges.
    

#### System Integration

- TPC arrays convert reactor thermal energy to $504\text{ MW}_{\text{e}}$ (Nimbus).
    
- Layer 1 stores bulk energy; Layer 2 stabilizes voltage; Layer 3 delivers instantaneous peak power to ISCD coils.
    
- **Mandatory Buffer:** $10\%$ unallocated capacitor buffer isolated for regenerative braking dumps.
    

#### Operational Notes

- TPC arrays continuously trickle-charge Layer 1 during normal operations.
    
- A $26\text{-second}$ full-power ISCD sprint consumes $1.414\text{ MWh}$ from storage ($195.84\text{ MW}$ deficit beyond TPC output).
    
- Total HESS mass remains negligible compared to reactor ballast ($10.5\text{ kt}$ for Nimbus).
    

## Segment 3 — Reaction Drive Integration & Thrust-Tier Power Allocations

Primary hybrid reactors supply energy as direct thermodynamic plasma for realspace propulsion and as TPC-converted electricity for general vessel grids and ISCD actuation.

```
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                            PROPULSION THRUST TIERS                              |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|  HIGH:  Magnetic Pinch Nuclear (Uranium)  --> Full Escape / Inter-System Burn  |
|  MED:   Hydrogen Plasma Thermal Burn      --> Standard Sub-Light Transit        |
|  LOW:   Cold Electron / Aux Ion Jet       --> Precision Station-Keeping / Docking |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### 3.1 Tier I: Magnetic Pinch Nuclear Drive (High Thrust / Heavy Escape)

- **Fuel:** $50\%\ \text{U-235/238}$ Eutectic Salt Slurry ($10\text{ t}$ baseline, Nimbus) injected into the D-T plasma pinch.
    
- **Dynamics:** Reactor consumes up to $100\%$ nominal core yield. Magnetic compression coils channel nuclear plasma out the aft Orion jet nozzle.
    
- **Mandate:** Permitted only for orbital escape, heavy gravitational maneuvers, or deep-space inter-system acceleration burns outside planetary Kármán boundaries.
    

### 3.2 Tier II: Hydrogen Plasma Injection (Medium Thrust / Sub-Light Cruising)

- **Fuel:** Compressed liquid hydrogen / water propellant stock.
    
- **Dynamics:** Reactor at $40\%\text{--}60\%$ core thermal capacity. Heat exchangers superheat hydrogen propellant for magnetic expansion.
    
- **Mandate:** Standard in-system transit, orbital insertion, and high-speed maneuvering within regulated flight corridors.
    

### 3.3 Tier III: Cold Electron & Auxiliary Ion Discharge (Low Thrust / Precision Station-Keeping)

- **Fuel:** Stored electron current / ionized noble gas.
    
- **Dynamics:** Reactor in idle maintenance mode ($5\%\text{--}15\%$ yield). Power drawn from TPC-charged main battery banks to drive electrostatic micro-thrusters.
    
- **Mandate:** Precision docking, fine attitude control, atmosphere-adjacent maneuvers, and harbor navigation.
    

### 3.4 ISCD Radial Stroke Power Interlock

When the ISCD is engaged, primary reaction drives lock into Tier III or Idle Mode.

- **Power Handover:** Reactor TPC generation redirected $100\%$ into charging ISCD capacitor banks.
    
- **Coil Pulse Requirement:** At $1\text{ kHz}$ baseline pulse rate, all $200\text{ rings}$ (Nimbus-class) draw $\sim 699.84\text{ MW}$ instantaneous peak power, demanding maximum discharge rates from TPC-buffered capacitor banks.
