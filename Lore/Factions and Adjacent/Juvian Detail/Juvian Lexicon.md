## Morphological & Affix Rules

### 1. Parenthetical Affix Notation `(prefix)`

Certain morphemes in Juvian are marked with parentheses to indicate they are optional prefixes that fundamentally change a word's meaning when attached. These are not simple negations but create distinct lexical items.

| **Base Word** | **Meaning** | **Prefixed Form** | **Resulting Meaning** | **Morphological Notes**                                                                |
| ------------- | ----------- | ----------------- | --------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `yok`         | new         | `aniyok`          | old                   | `ani-` creates a distinct semantic category rather than a literal negation ("not new") |

### 2. Freestanding Reference & The Shell Concept (`Shell(pronoun)`)

Juvian lacks independent, fundamental referential pronouns (such as _I_, _you_, _he/she_, or _this/that_). In Juvian grammar, all freestanding reference is derived through the **Shell Concept** using the root noun **`soq`** (/soq/, meaning "body," "carapace," or "physical envelope").
To express referential concepts, `soq` undergoes **forced pronominal conjugation** (`Shell`-flexion), elevating a concrete physical noun into a functional grammatical pronoun (`Shell(pronoun)`).

#### `Shell(pronoun)` Conjugation Paradigm Table

| **Shell Concept Name** | **Grammatical Function**                     | **Juvian Form (IPA)**  | **Root Components**                             | **Literal Translation & Etymology** |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| **Self-Shell**         | 1st Person Singular ("I / Me")               | `soq-ʔ` / `soq-ʔok`    | `soq` + `ʔ` (Primary Unit)                      | _"This primary physical envelope"_  |
| **Addressed Shell**    | 2nd Person Singular ("You")                  | `soq-quɹ` / `soq-vkʊl` | `soq` + `quɹ`/`vkʊl` (Optic/Acoustic Receptors) | _"The observed physical envelope"_  |
| **External Shell**     | 3rd Person / Distal ("He / She / It / That") | `soq-kiɹ` / `soq-vʒ`   | `soq` + `kiɹ` (There) / `vʒ` (Out)              | _"Outer physical envelope"_         |
| **Proximal Shell**     | Demonstrative ("This / This One")            | `soq-vɹ`               | `soq` + `vɹ` (Here)                             | _"Here-envelope"_                   |
| **Collective Shell**   | Plural Reference ("We / They / You All")     | `soq-aʎiʒ`             | `soq` + `aʎiʒ` (All)                            | _"All-envelopes"_                   |
